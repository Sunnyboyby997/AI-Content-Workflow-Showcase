这个项目解决什么

海外 DTC 内容团队最头疼的三件事，用一套可复用的 AI 工作流解决。

1 文案有 AI 味、没人味
靠一套「去AI化」Skill：内置中英文禁用词表（seamless / 赋能 / 极致…），把空泛形容词逼成具体数字和场景。
2 内容生产低效、不 SOP 化
把「选题 → 文案 → 脚本 → 复盘」沉淀成 4 个 Skill + 4 个 Agent，批量处理文件夹级内容资产。
3 品牌知识散落、难复用
统一接入品牌知识库（调性 / 卖点 / 人群 / 竞品 / SEO词库 / 本地化参数 / 复盘数据），每次生产都先读知识库。

4 个 Skill（可复用 SOP）

每个 Skill = 一套沉淀下来的标准作业流程，Claude 按 description 自动触发。

S1 brand-copy
去AI化品牌文案
  • 先读品牌知识库，缺信息就问、不瞎编
  • 内置中英文 AI 味禁用词表（60+ 词）
  • 短句、给具体数字、诚实写产品边界
  • 强制输出格式：标题+3段+3要点
S2 seo-geo-builder
SEO & GEO 本地化内容
  • 关键词意图 → 内容类型（信息/交易型）
  • 站内 SEO：Title/Meta/Schema/内链
  • GEO：FAQ/列表/对比结构，让 AI 搜索引用
  • 多地区本地化（语言/货币/单位）
S3 short-video-script
海外短视频 / TikTok 脚本
  • HOOK / 场景 / 演示 / CTA 四段结构
  • 每条含「口播词 + 屏上文字」
  • 前 3 秒必须给钩子
  • 复用 brand-copy 的去AI化词表
S4 content-retrospect
内容复盘 & 资产沉淀
  • 数据 → 归因 → 结论 → 动作四步
  • 爆款拆解 + 扑街归因
  • 把规律写回 SOP，形成迭代闭环
  • 沉淀可复用素材资产

All-day battery. A19 speed. The honest part: you'll pay for it.

You unplug at 7am and don't think about a charger until you're home after dark. The iPhone 17 runs the A19 chip, and the battery is rated for up to 30 hours of video. Our own test: 24 and a half hours of continuous streaming before it died. On a normal day — calls, scrolling, some games — you land around 30% by 11pm.

The screen is 6.3 inches at 120Hz. That's the same ProMotion refresh rate the Pro models used to keep to themselves. It's smooth, and it's finally on the base model.

Now the part most pages skip. Charging tops out at 30W. Some Android flagships do 100W and fill in 15 minutes. If you're the type who forgets to charge overnight and needs a full top-up in a coffee shop, this will annoy you. It also starts at $799 — this is not a budget phone.

Reddit 种草帖（真实体验）content_output/social_posts/reddit-review.md
3 months with the iPhone 17 — the parts nobody puts in the ad

Paid $799, had it about 3 months. Writing this because I nearly didn't buy it, and the reasons I nearly didn't are worth knowing.

The good:

  • Battery is the real deal. On a normal day — messaging, reddit, YouTube, some light games — I go 7am to about 11pm and land around 30%. One charge a day actually holds up.
  • A19 chip is fast. Apps open instantly, no stutter, even with a dozen tabs and a game in the background.
  • 120Hz screen. This used to be a Pro-only thing. Scrolling feels noticeably better than my old 60Hz phone.

The not-so-good (the honesty part):

  • Charging. It's 30W. My friend's Android does 100W and fills in 15 minutes while this takes about an hour. If you forget to charge overnight, you'll feel it.
  • Price. $799 for a base phone is a lot, and 256GB is the floor — no microSD.

Who it's for: you want a phone that lasts a full day and you're already in — or fine joining — the Apple ecosystem. You keep phones 3+ years and care about software updates.

Who it's not for: anyone who needs 15-minute fast charging, or who wants the cheapest phone that does the job.
