# TikTok 脚本：iPhone 17 凭什么比安卓贵？拆给你看

## HOOK（0–3s）
口播词：iPhone 17 is $799. A Galaxy S25 is about the same price. So what are you actually paying for?
屏上文字：$799 · 钱花在哪

## 场景（3–10s）
口播词：It's not the chip — both are fast. It's not the screen — both are 120Hz. It's what happens after you buy it.
屏上文字：真正的差异在买之后

## 演示（10–20s）
口播词：Apple pushes updates for 5 to 7 years. The phone keeps its value — after two years it's worth more of what you paid than most Androids. And if you have a Mac, everything syncs. That's the money.
屏上文字：5–7 年更新 / 保值率高 / 生态无缝

## 边界（诚实说缺点）
口播词：But if you swap phones every year and live in Android, none of that helps you. A cheaper Android is the better deal for you — don't pay for years you won't use.
屏上文字：年年换机？安卓更划算

## CTA（最后 3s）
口播词：Do you keep your phone 3+ years, or swap every year? Tell me below.
屏上文字：你是哪一类

---
脚本自查：钩子用「价格相同却各有说法」制造反常识；把「贵」拆成「更新周期 + 保值率 + 生态」三个具体点；主动写「年年换机就别买 iPhone」这一边界，不硬吹。
