# TikTok 脚本：同配置凭什么便宜 XX% 拆解

## HOOK（0–3s）
口播词：Same chip as a $【XXX】 phone. We charge $【XX】. Where did the money go?
屏上文字：同芯片 · 便宜【XX】%

## 场景（3–10s）
口播词：It's not magic, and it's not "you get what you pay for" either. It's about where the money goes.
屏上文字：钱花在哪

## 演示（10–20s）
口播词：You pay for the chip, the battery, the screen — the stuff you touch every day. Those are the same as Brand A. What we cut is the camera. Mid-range sensor, day shots fine, night shots average. And no brand tax — that's the 【XX】% right there.
屏上文字：同款芯片 / 同款电池 / 省了相机 / 省了品牌税

## CTA（最后 3s）
口播词：Would you trade a flagship camera to save 【XX】%? Tell me below.
屏上文字：你会怎么选

---
脚本自查：钩子用价格差制造反常识；有具体数字（$【XXX】 vs $【XX】、便宜【XX】%）；有 CTA；把「便宜来自砍掉相机 + 不交品牌税」这一产品边界讲清楚，不遮丑。
