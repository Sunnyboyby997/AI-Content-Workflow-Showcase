# TikTok 脚本：一天一充实测

## HOOK（0–3s）
口播词：Phone left the house at 100% this morning. Not charging it till I get home. Let's see what's left.
屏上文字：100% → ?

## 场景（3–10s）
口播词：Full day of classes. No outlet at my seat. Group chat all day, an hour of videos on the bus home.
屏上文字：无插座 · 一天

## 演示（10–20s）
口播词：It's 12:40am. Battery's at 【XX】%. That's 【XX】mAh doing its job. One charge, one full day, no plugging in between.
屏上文字：12:40am · 【XX】%

## 边界（诚实说缺点，并进演示或 CTA 前）
口播词：Quick honest note — the camera's mid-range. Day shots fine, night shots average. Battery's the star here, not the camera.
屏上文字：相机中端 · 夜景一般

## CTA（最后 3s）
口播词：What did your battery look like today? Drop it in the comments.
屏上文字：评论区晒你的电量

---
脚本自查：钩子 3 秒内抛出悬念（100% 出门）；有具体数字（【XX】%、【XX】mAh、时间点）；有 CTA；写明了相机短板这一产品边界。
