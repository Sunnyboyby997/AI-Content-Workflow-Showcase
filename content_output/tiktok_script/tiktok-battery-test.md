# TikTok 脚本：一天一充实测（iPhone 17）

口播词：Phone left the house at 100% this morning. Not charging it till I get home. Let's see what's left.

## 场景（3–10s）
口播词：Full day — classes, no outlet at my seat, group chat all day, an hour of videos on the way home.
屏上文字：无插座 · 一天

## 演示（10–20s）
口播词：It's 11:40pm. Battery's at 31%. The A19 chip sips power — Apple rates it for 30 hours of video, and in a straight streaming test it hit 24 and a half. One charge, one full day.
屏上文字：11:40pm · 31%

## 边界（诚实说缺点，放在演示后）
口播词：One honest thing — charging is 30W, slower than a lot of Androids. Overnight it's a non-issue; if you need a 15-minute top-up, this isn't it.
屏上文字：充电 30W · 比安卓旗舰慢

## CTA（最后 3s）
口播词：What did your battery look like today? Drop it in the comments.
屏上文字：评论区晒你的电量
